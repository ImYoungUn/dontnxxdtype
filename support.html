Set up the keyboard

  1. Open the Settings app and go to General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard.
  2. Choose DontNxxdType.
  3. Tap DontNxxdType in the list and turn on Allow Full Access (needed only for the AI).
  4. In any app, press and hold the 🌐 key and pick DontNxxdType.

How to use it

  1. Type what you mean in Your Message. It doesn't need to be polite or perfect.
  2. Optional: tap the clipboard button to add the message you're replying to.
  3. Tap AI, then Use to type the reply into your chat.
  4. Tap the ⚙️ key to choose your favorite tones.

FAQ

The AI button says it needs Full Access.
Turn on Allow Full Access as in step 3 above. iOS requires it for any keyboard that uses the internet.

The AI couldn't be reached.
Check your internet connection and try again. You can still tap Use to insert your draft as is.

How do I switch back to my usual keyboard?
Tap the 🌐 key.

What happens to my text?
See the Privacy Policy. In short: text is sent only when you tap AI, and we don't store it.
