Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

DontNxxdType is an iPhone keyboard that rewrites a rough draft into a polished message with AI. This policy explains what data the app and keyboard handle and why.

The short version

  • We do not log your keystrokes or read what you type into other apps.
  • Text leaves your phone only when you tap AI, and only the text in the keyboard's Your Message box.
  • We don't store that text on our servers, and we don't use it for advertising or profiling.
  • No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, no tracking.

What is sent when you tap AI

When you tap the AI button, the keyboard sends the following over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server:

  • the draft you typed in Your Message;
  • the message you are replying to, if you pasted one with the clipboard button;
  • the tone tags you selected (for example "Warm" or "To Boss").

Our server, hosted on Amazon Web Services in Seoul, passes this text to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the rewritten message and returns the result to your keyboard. Our server does not save the text or write it to logs. Anthropic processes it as our service provider under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.

Please don't put passwords, card numbers or other sensitive information into Your Message.

Full Access

iOS asks you to "Allow Full Access" before a keyboard can use the network. DontNxxdType needs it only to reach the AI. The keyboard does not use Full Access to collect anything else. Without Full Access you can still type and insert text; only the AI rewrite is unavailable.

Data stored on your device

To keep your work when you switch apps, the keyboard saves your current draft and last result on your iPhone and clears them after you insert a reply or after 24 hours. Your favorite tone tags are also saved on your device. This data never leaves your phone unless you tap AI as described above. Deleting the app removes it.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data. Text you send for rewriting is processed only by the providers named above (Amazon Web Services and Anthropic) to provide the feature.

Children

DontNxxdType is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
